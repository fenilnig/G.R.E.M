Layout rule: keys, four dials, haptic dial and OLED hang from the sloped lid. Power parts sit on the floor at the tall rear. The 10 × 10 cm board carries the ESP32-S3, MCP23017 and DRV2605L in the middle, where the lid is high enough to clear them. The OLED and KY-040 sizes come from the makers' drawings. The MCP23017, DRV2605L, TP4056, XL6009, ESP32 and rocker sizes are still estimates.

Not numbered: soldering iron, multimeter, strippers, cutters, tweezers, desoldering braid, and the XT60 plugs and LM2596 buck board (rover parts).
